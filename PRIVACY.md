# SongLumen Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

SongLumen is a macOS menu-bar companion for the Music app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Data collection

SongLumen does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store personal data on external servers.

To show its now-playing card, SongLumen reads the currently playing track's title, artist, album, and artwork from the Music app on your Mac. This information is used only on your device to display the card and is not sent to TerraLayer or any third party.

## Local preferences

SongLumen may store simple preferences on your Mac, such as whether the app should launch at login. These preferences remain on your device.

## Third-party services

SongLumen does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party data collection services.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue at [github.com/terralayer/SongLumen](https://github.com/terralayer/SongLumen).
